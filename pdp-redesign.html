Product Page Redesign

Gopuff's product display page had accessibility gaps and didn't show products well. Before redesigning it, the team needed to know what good looks like.

RoleUX Research Intern
WhenSummer 2022
Scope12 apps reviewed
MethodsCompetitive analysis
At a glance2 min read
Problem

The product page had accessibility gaps and sold products poorly.

What I did

Benchmarked 12 apps against Baymard research and presented a 25-slide deck.

Result

The redesigned page went live in August 2023.

The askCompetitive analysisWhat I foundThe presentationThe outcome

The ask

The team wanted a clearer, better-looking product page. I started with a competitive analysis so the redesign was based on evidence, not opinions.

Competitive analysis

I reviewed Gopuff's current page against 9 direct competitors (Instacart, Shipt, Grubhub/Seamless, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo, Foxtrot, Favor, and Drizly) and 3 category leaders (Amazon, Target, and Chewy).

I scored each on value props, image and text size, layout, and product info, using Baymard Institute's e-commerce research as the benchmark.

What I found

  • Photos: most apps gave product images a big share of the screen.
  • Titles: some kept them short, others added count, weight, volume, and brand.
  • Price and rewards: placement was all over the place. Some led with price, others put rewards right next to it.
  • Out of stock: some changed the add button to "Unavailable," others showed a message under the header.
  • Value props: discounts, percent off, popularity, and availability all showed up.
  • Product info: ranged from full ingredients and nutrition to short, punchy descriptions.
  • Brand info: some linked it with a subheading, others with a chevron or button.
  • Alcohol: some apps added ABV, tasting notes, and food pairings.

The presentation

I pulled the findings, insights, and recommendations into a 25-slide deck for stakeholders.

The deck I presented to stakeholders

The outcome

The research shaped concepts that gave images more space, tightened titles, moved key info where people expected it, and sharpened the value props.
