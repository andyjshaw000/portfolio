UW CSE 440 · Research + Design

STRELMO

STRELMO, the Stress Less Mouse, uses a cortisol sensor developed at UCLA to read stress hormones through sweat. It sends that data to a desktop app so people can see how their stress changes over time.

UsersUniversity students

The problem

We started with workplace stress, then narrowed our focus to university students after our first round of research. The plan was to interview UW students, then run heuristic evaluations and usability tests with outside participants.

Interviews

We interviewed UW students about how they handle stress, how they'd feel about tracking it, and our early ideas, then coded the transcripts for themes.

Interview notes

People had very different stressors and ways of coping. They wanted to see patterns over time, they liked routines and reminders, and they needed the information to be simple and clear.

Four concepts

  1. Phone app and cortisol watch that tracks stress, sends alerts, and points to resources.
  2. Cortisol meter, a discreet wearable that flags high-stress moments.
  3. Responsive diary and watch that connects journal entries to stress levels.
  4. Desktop app and cortisol mouse. We picked this one because nothing like it existed, and a mouse has a lot of skin contact during computer work.

Usability testing

We tested realistic tasks like checking your stress, finding a stress-relief activity, and getting around the app. We started with paper prototypes and moved to digital ones. Every issue got a severity rating, and we fixed the critical ones first.

The desktop app after usability testing

A heuristic evaluation with other students caught more issues, also ranked by severity.

Final design

What testing changed:

  • Added vibration alerts alongside the visual ones.
  • Made the Stats tab much clearer.
  • Split Resources into separate Reminders and Activities pages.
  • Switched to a color palette that works for people with low vision.
  • Found that the mouse changing color might not get noticed, so we suggested adding sound.
