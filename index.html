01Featured project

From a group chat problem to a live app on the App Store.

Side project · iOS · Live

kndi

Plan festivals with your friends.

Planning a festival with a group usually means a messy group chat, five screenshots of the set times, and nobody knowing where to meet. kndi puts all of that in one place. Every event gets a group with chat, polls, a shared schedule, and an album, and you get a heads up when two sets you picked overlap.

It also brings kandi trading into the app. You design digital beads and trade them by scanning each other's codes in person. The goal was never to replace the real thing. It's a way to keep the memory of who you met.

  • ProductDecided what to build and what to cut, based on how my friends and I actually plan shows.
  • BuildBuilt and shipped the iOS app with a small group of friends, using AI coding tools to move fast.
  • GrowthWrote the go-to-market plan, focused on local network effects and Insomniac events, and worked on App Store discoverability.
